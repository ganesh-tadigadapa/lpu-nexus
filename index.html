Campus Services

All essential tools and resources for a smoother campus life.

LPU Touch

Making education and administrative processes more efficient.

How to use
  1. Install the LPU Touch app.
  2. Login with user credentials.
  3. Access your academics and your syllabus.
LPU Live

Communication, collaboration and engagement on a single platform.

How to use
  1. Install the LPU Live app from the link.
  2. Login with user credentials.
  3. Contact your academic teachers and peers.
LPU.in

Access academic records, schedules and more.

How to use
  1. Click the link on this card.
  2. Open lpu.in and enter your username and password.
  3. Access academic records, schedules and more.
Smart Q

Order in advance, save time by avoiding the queue.

How to use
  1. Install the Smart Q app.
  2. Enter your details.
  3. Order your favourite food and enjoy your meal.
LPU Golf Cart

Book an LPU golf cart for easy transportation.

How to use
  1. Install the LPU Golf Cart app.
  2. Open the app.
  3. Enter the driver ID.
  4. Select your pickup and drop-off locations.
My Horse

Rent bicycles for short trips around campus.

How to use
  1. Install the My Horse application.
  2. Enter your personal details.
  3. Top up your wallet.
  4. Track the nearest cycle around you.
  5. Scan the QR code and enjoy your rides.
University Management System

Access academic records, schedules and more.

How to use
  1. Click the link on this card.
  2. Open the UMS and enter your username and password.
  3. Access academic records, schedules and more.
LPU Dashboard

Web-based system managing academic activities such as attendance and assignments.

How to use
  1. Click the link to open the LPU Dashboard.
  2. Login with your credentials.
  3. The dashboard helps with your personal and academic queries.
Food Delivery

Order food from campus cafeterias and restaurants.

How to use
  1. Install the Domino's app.
  2. Select your location and your favourite item.
  3. Select your payment option.
  4. Enjoy your food.
